About

Two different educations, one way of working

How a printing floor in Colombo and a cyber security degree in Perth ended up teaching the same lessons.

I'm a final-year Bachelor of Computer Science student majoring in Cyber Security at Edith Cowan University in Perth, with earlier academic experience in Sri Lanka. I'm a quick learner who works well under deadline pressure — something I picked up long before university, running production floors where a missed handoff meant a missed delivery.

Before and alongside my studies, I spent several years in operations and people management at Art-E Printing, one of the oldest and largest printing houses in Sri Lanka — coordinating everything from customer enquiries to production schedules and board-level reporting. That background shapes how I approach technical problems: I look for the process behind the failure, not just the fix.
